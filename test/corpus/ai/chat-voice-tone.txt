Voice and tone are closely related, but they're not the same thing. Here's a simple way to think about it:

**Voice is who you are.** It's the consistent personality of your writing. A brand's voice might be friendly, confident, and a little playful, and that stays the same whether you're writing a homepage or an error message.

**Tone is how you adapt.** Tone shifts depending on the situation and the reader's emotional state. You wouldn't use the same tone to celebrate a customer's first purchase as you would to explain that their account has been locked.

**An analogy**

Think of how you speak. Your voice is consistent: your friends would recognize it anywhere. But your tone changes depending on whether you're at a birthday party or comforting someone at a funeral.

**Examples**

- **Celebratory tone:** "You did it! Your application is complete."
- **Serious tone:** "We couldn't process your payment. Please check your card details and try again."

Both examples can share the same underlying voice: clear, human, and helpful.

**Why it matters**

Defining your voice creates consistency and builds trust across every touchpoint. Adjusting your tone shows empathy and ensures your message resonates with readers in the moment.
