Unpopular opinion: If your audience needs a dictionary, you've already lost them.

I've reviewed hundreds of government and corporate documents over the years. The pattern is always the same.

Long sentences. Passive voice. Acronyms nobody explains.

And then everyone wonders why people don't read it.

Plain language isn't "dumbing it down." It's respect.

✅ Respect for your reader's time.
✅ Respect for their intelligence.
✅ Respect for the fact that they have 47 other things competing for their attention.

Here's a simple test I use:

Could a busy parent understand this on their phone while waiting in the school pickup line?

If not, rewrite it.

The best writers I know aren't the ones who use the biggest words. They're the ones who make complex ideas feel simple.

Clarity is a superpower. Use it.
