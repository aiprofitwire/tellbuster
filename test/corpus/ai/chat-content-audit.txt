A content audit is a systematic review of all the content on your website. It helps you understand what you have, how it's performing, and what needs to change. Here's a step-by-step guide:

**Step 1: Define your goals**
Are you preparing for a redesign, improving SEO, or cutting outdated pages? Your goals will shape what you measure.

**Step 2: Create an inventory**
Use a crawler like Screaming Frog to list every URL on your site. Export the results to a spreadsheet.

**Step 3: Gather data**
For each page, add columns for things like page views, last updated date, owner, and word count. Tools like Google Analytics can help you fill these in.

**Step 4: Evaluate the content**
Review each page against your goals. A common approach is to label each page as:
- **Keep:** Accurate and useful
- **Update:** Valuable but outdated
- **Merge:** Overlaps with another page
- **Remove:** No longer needed

**Step 5: Make a plan**
Prioritize the changes based on impact and effort, and assign owners.

A content audit can feel overwhelming at first, especially for large sites, but it's one of the most valuable exercises you can do. It provides a clear picture of your content landscape and lays the groundwork for a more effective, user-friendly website.
