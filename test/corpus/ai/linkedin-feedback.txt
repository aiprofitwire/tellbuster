The best feedback I ever received was 11 words long.

"Your ideas are strong. Your slides are hiding them. Simplify."

It stung for about an hour.

Then it changed how I present forever.

Here's what made it great feedback:

🎯 It was specific. Not "good job" or "needs work."
⏱️ It was timely. Right after the meeting, not in a review six months later.
🤝 It was kind. It started with something true and positive.
🛠️ It was actionable. I knew exactly what to do next.

So many of us avoid giving feedback because we're afraid of hurting feelings. But withholding feedback isn't kindness. It's a missed opportunity for someone to grow.

Next time you notice something a colleague could do better, ask yourself:

Would I want to know?

If the answer is yes, tell them. Kindly, clearly, and soon.

Your team will thank you.
