Thank you to everyone who joined yesterday's journey mapping workshop! The energy in the room was fantastic, and I'm genuinely impressed by the insights we uncovered together.

Here's a quick summary of what we accomplished:

**What we mapped**
We mapped the end-to-end experience of a first-time applicant, from discovering our program to receiving their first payment.

**Key insights**
- **The waiting is the hardest part.** Applicants often wait two to three weeks without hearing anything, which leads to anxiety and repeat calls.
- **Documents are a major barrier.** Many people don't have the paperwork we ask for and aren't sure where to get it.
- **Staff are working around the system.** Caseworkers have created their own spreadsheets to track applications because the current tool doesn't meet their needs.

**Top opportunities**
1. Send automatic status updates at each stage.
2. Create a simple checklist of required documents.
3. Explore improvements to the caseworker tool.
