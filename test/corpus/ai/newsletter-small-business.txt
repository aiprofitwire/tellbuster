**This month's big idea: Get to know your numbers**

Many small business owners are passionate about their products but feel less comfortable with the financial side of things. The good news? You don't need to be an accountant to understand the basics.

Start with three numbers:
1. **Monthly revenue:** How much money is coming in?
2. **Monthly expenses:** How much is going out?
3. **Cash on hand:** How many months could you operate if sales stopped?

Tracking these every month can help you spot problems early and make smarter decisions.

**Quick tip**

Set aside one hour on the first Monday of every month for a "money date" with your business. Grab a coffee, review your numbers, and plan ahead.
