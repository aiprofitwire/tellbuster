Most businesses need a license from the city or county where they operate. Some also need a state license, and a few types of work need a federal one.

Start with your city or county website. Search for "business license" and the name of your city. The page usually lists what you need and the fee. If you can't find it, call the city clerk's office.

You will usually need your business name and address, your owner information, and a tax ID. If you are a sole owner with no employees, your Social Security number may be enough. Otherwise, get a free Employer Identification Number from the IRS first.

Check the zoning rules before you sign a lease or start work from home. Some areas don't allow certain businesses, or they limit signs, parking or customer visits.

Licenses expire. Most need to be renewed every year. Put the renewal date in your calendar now.

If your work involves food, alcohol, childcare, construction or health care, expect extra permits and inspections. Ask the licensing office which ones apply to you.
