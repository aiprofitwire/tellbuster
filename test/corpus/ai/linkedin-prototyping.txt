Stop building. Start prototyping.

Last quarter, my team was about to spend 8 weeks building a new onboarding flow.

Instead, we spent 2 days on a clickable prototype.

We tested it with 6 users.

The result? 4 of them got stuck on the very first screen.

That prototype saved us roughly 7 weeks of engineering time and a painful launch.

Here's why prototyping is one of the most underrated skills in product development:

→ It turns opinions into evidence.
→ It makes ideas tangible for stakeholders.
→ It fails fast and cheap, before the stakes are high.
→ It gets everyone aligned around something real.

The goal isn't perfection. A paper sketch can be enough to spark the right conversation.

Remember: the most expensive way to test an idea is to build it.
