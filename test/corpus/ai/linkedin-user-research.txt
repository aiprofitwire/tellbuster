I used to think I knew what our users wanted.

I was wrong.

Early in my career, I spent six months building a feature I was sure would be a game-changer. We launched it with a big announcement.

Nobody used it. 📉

Here's what I learned:

🔍 Assumptions are not insights. Your gut is a starting point, not a strategy.

🗣️ Talk to users early. Not after launch. Not "when we have time." Now.

👂 Listen more than you pitch. The best research sessions are the ones where you barely speak.

🔁 Test, learn, repeat. Every conversation makes the next decision a little less of a guess.

Today, my team talks to at least five users every sprint. It's not glamorous. But it's the single biggest reason our products actually get used.

The truth is, your users are already telling you what they need. The question is whether you're listening.
