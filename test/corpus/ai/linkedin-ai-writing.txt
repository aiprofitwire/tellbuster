AI won't replace writers.

But writers who use AI will replace writers who don't.

I've been using AI tools in my writing workflow for the past year. Here's my honest take:

What AI is great at:
✅ Beating the blank page
✅ Summarizing long documents
✅ Suggesting alternative phrasings
✅ Catching typos and awkward sentences

What AI still struggles with:
❌ Original insight
❌ Your unique voice and lived experience
❌ Knowing what your audience actually cares about
❌ Judgment about what to leave out

The magic happens when you combine the two. Let AI handle the heavy lifting on first drafts and research, and bring your own perspective, stories, and taste to the final product.

The future of writing isn't human vs. machine. It's human with machine.
