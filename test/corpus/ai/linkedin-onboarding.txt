A new hire decides whether to stay in their first 90 days.

Most companies spend those 90 days on paperwork.

I've seen it happen too many times. Someone talented joins, sits through a week of compliance videos, gets a laptop that doesn't work, and waits for someone to tell them what to do.

By month three, they're already updating their résumé.

Great onboarding looks different:

👋 A welcome message before day one
🧭 A clear 30-60-90 day plan
🤝 A buddy who isn't their manager
🎯 One meaningful project in the first two weeks
💬 Regular check-ins that are actually about how they're doing

Onboarding isn't an HR task. It's a leadership responsibility.

Because the way you welcome people tells them everything about how you'll treat them.
