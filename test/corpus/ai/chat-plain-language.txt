Plain language matters for government websites because it directly impacts whether people can access the services and information they need. Here are some key reasons:

1. **Accessibility for everyone.** Government services are meant for the entire public, including people with limited literacy, non-native speakers, and people with cognitive disabilities. Clear writing ensures no one is left behind.

2. **Fewer errors and calls.** When forms and instructions are easy to understand, people make fewer mistakes. This reduces the burden on call centers and caseworkers.

3. **Trust and transparency.** Jargon-heavy language can make government feel distant or even intimidating. Plain language fosters trust by showing respect for the reader's time.

4. **It's the law.** In the United States, the Plain Writing Act of 2010 requires federal agencies to use clear communication that the public can understand and use.

5. **Better outcomes.** Studies consistently show that plain language improves comprehension for all readers, including experts, who appreciate being able to find information quickly.

In short, plain language isn't about "dumbing down" content. It's about respecting your audience and making sure your message lands. By prioritizing clarity, government agencies can deliver more effective, equitable, and user-centered services.
