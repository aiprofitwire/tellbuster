    HOW IT WORKS

    Write like you. Tellbuster points at the rest.

    1. 1

      Write as usual

      Draft your post, email or reply on LinkedIn, X, Gmail or any site. Nothing changes about how you type.

    2. 2

      See what stands out

      A small badge counts the phrases that read as AI. Red is a strong tell, amber a common one, gray a style note.

    3. 3

      Fix it your way

      Each card explains why readers notice the phrase and suggests a plainer version. You decide what to keep.

    A LINTER, NOT A DETECTOR

    It never says "this is AI."

    AI detectors guess, and they wrongly accuse real people. Tellbuster does something simpler and more honest: it shows the exact phrases readers link to machine writing and explains each one. People use these phrases too. You see the pattern and make the call.

    What a detector says: "87% likely AI-generated"

    What Tellbuster says: "3 phrases might read as AI. Here is why."
